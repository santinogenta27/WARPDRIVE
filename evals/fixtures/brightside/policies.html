Brightside Outdoor Co. — Returns and refunds policy

Brightside Outdoor Co. is a fictional outdoor gear store used only for testing. Revised November 2026.

Return window

You can return any unused item within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be clean, unworn and in their original packaging with tags attached.

  • Final sale items, gift cards and personalized products can't be returned.
  • Returns are free with the prepaid label we email you.
  • Worn or used items are reviewed by our team and may be refused.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item. Every refund is reviewed and approved by a person on our team before it's issued.

Exchanges

Size and color exchanges are free within 45 days. We ship the new item as soon as the original is scanned by the carrier.

Late deliveries

If an order arrives later than its estimated date, we can offer a coupon of up to 10% off your next order or free express shipping. We never offer larger discounts for delays.
