# Warranty

Every Northwind Outfitters product has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date. Outerwear (jackets and vests) and footwear have a 2-year warranty.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship: broken zippers, seams coming apart, delamination, sole separation and faulty snaps or buttons.

It doesn't cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, or alterations.

To make a warranty claim, contact us with your order number and a photo of the defect. A member of our team reviews it within 2 business days. Depending on the defect, we repair the item, replace it with the same product, or refund it if it can't be repaired or replaced.

Warranty repairs and replacements are free, including shipping both ways.
