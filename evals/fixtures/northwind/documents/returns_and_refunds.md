# Returns and refunds

You can return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it's unworn, unwashed and has its original tags.

Returns are free: we email you a prepaid return label. Drop the package at any UPS location.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once the refund is issued, it takes 5 to 7 business days to show up, depending on your bank.

Refunds of $100 or less are issued right away by our assistant. Refunds over $100 are reviewed by a member of our team, usually within one business day, and you get a message as soon as it's approved.

Items marked "Final sale" can't be returned or refunded.

If an item arrived damaged or defective, see our warranty: we repair it, replace it or refund it at no cost.

You can cancel an order at no cost before it ships. After it ships, you can return it once it arrives.
