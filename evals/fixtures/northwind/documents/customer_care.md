# Customer care

Our virtual assistant answers 24/7 in this chat. It can check your orders, change a shipping address, cancel an order before it ships, start refunds and size exchanges, and answer questions about our policies.

Our customer care team is available Monday to Friday from 8 am to 6 pm Pacific time, and Saturday from 9 am to 1 pm Pacific time. If you ask for a person, a member of the team replies in this same chat.

Northwind Outfitters is based in Portland, Oregon. We don't have physical stores: we sell only online.

To protect your account, our assistant verifies your identity with a 6-digit code sent to the email you used for your purchase before sharing order details or making changes.
