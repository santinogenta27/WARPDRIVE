# Discounts and coupons

New subscribers to our newsletter get 10% off their first order.

When something goes wrong with an order (it arrives late, an item is missing, the wrong size was shipped), our customer care team and our assistant can offer a courtesy coupon of up to 10% off the next order. Courtesy coupons are valid for 60 days and are applied at checkout.

Discounts above 10% aren't possible, and we can't apply a discount to an order that was already placed. When a customer asks for more, the alternative we can offer is free express shipping on the next order.

Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons can't be combined with sale prices.

We don't offer price adjustments after purchase.
