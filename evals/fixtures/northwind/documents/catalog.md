## Size guide

Tops and outerwear (chest): XS 34–35 in, S 36–38 in, M 39–41 in, L 42–44 in, XL 45–47 in.

Our jackets have a regular fit with room for a sweater underneath. If you're between sizes, choose the larger size for jackets and vests and the smaller size for sweaters.

Merino sweaters can shrink if machine-dried: wash them cold and lay them flat to dry.
