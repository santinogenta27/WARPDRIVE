# Shipping

Northwind Outfitters ships to every address in the United States and Canada from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

Orders placed before 2 pm Pacific time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed later, on weekends or on holidays leave on the next business day.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $6.95. Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95. You can choose express at checkout. If your order hasn't shipped yet, our team can upgrade it to express for you.

We ship with UPS and USPS. As soon as your order leaves the warehouse you get an email with the carrier and the tracking number, and you can always ask our assistant for the status of your order.

You can change the shipping address of an order until it ships. After it ships, the address can no longer be changed.

## Late orders

The estimated delivery date is an estimate from the carrier. If an order is late because of the carrier (weather, a hub delay), it usually arrives within 1 to 3 business days of the original estimate, and the order shows the new estimated delivery date.

If your order is late, we're sorry. You can choose how we make it right:

- Wait for the new delivery date and get a courtesy coupon of up to 10% off your next order.
- Get free express shipping on your next order.
- If the order still hasn't arrived 5 business days after the original estimate, we refund it in full or ship a replacement at no cost.

International shipping outside the United States and Canada isn't available yet.
